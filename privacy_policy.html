Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2024

This Privacy Policy describes how GameApp ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your personal information when you use our mobile application GameApp (the "App") and related services.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Device Information: Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers (such as IMEI, Android ID), IP address, network type, and device settings.
  • Usage Information: How you interact with the App, including game progress, levels completed, time spent, and features used.
  • Location Information: Approximate location based on IP address (optional, if enabled by user).
  • Contact Information: Email address (if provided for support or account creation).

1.2 Non-Personal Information

We also collect non-personal information that cannot be used to identify you, such as aggregate usage data, crash logs, and performance metrics.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To provide and maintain the App's functionality
  • To personalize your experience and improve the App
  • To display advertisements through ad networks
  • To analyze usage patterns and optimize performance
  • To provide customer support
  • To comply with legal obligations

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We may share your information with third-party service providers to help us operate the App. These include:

The App integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK to display advertisements. Google may collect device identifiers, usage data, location information, and other data for ad targeting and analytics purposes. For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

AppLovin MAX SDK

The App integrates the AppLovin MAX SDK, which serves as an ad mediation platform that connects to multiple ad networks. AppLovin may collect device identifiers, usage data, and location information for ad optimization and targeting. For more information, please see AppLovin's Privacy Policy: https://www.applovin.com/privacy/

Huawei Ads SDK

The App integrates the Huawei Ads SDK to display advertisements. Huawei may collect device identifiers, usage data, and location information for ad targeting purposes. For more information, please see Huawei's Privacy Policy: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/

The App integrates Huawei Push Kit to provide push notification services. Huawei may collect device identifiers and push token information to deliver notifications. For more information, please see Huawei's Privacy Policy: https://consumer.huawei.com/en/privacy/privacy-policy/

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM)

The App may integrate Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) to provide push notification services. Your personal information is stored on secure servers and protected by industry-standard security measures. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.
